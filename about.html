About the Owner

Jeremy Brown - JMC Brown Inc

Jeremy Brown is the owner and operator of JMC Brown Inc, a fully insured lawn care and landscaping company dedicated to delivering reliable, high-quality work on every job.

With a strong background in operating modern equipment and a hands-on approach, Jeremy takes pride in treating every property as if it were his own. Whether it’s a simple weekly mowing route, installing a retaining wall, solving a drainage problem, or cleaning up a site, the goal is always the same: clean results, fair pricing, and work that holds up.

JMC Brown Inc is equipped with professional-grade Kubota machinery and attachments, allowing the company to handle everything from fine landscaping details to heavier excavation and grading work. Being fully insured gives customers peace of mind on every project.

  • Fully insured operations
  • Modern equipment and quality materials
